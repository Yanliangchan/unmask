How it works

From a username to a defensible answer

  1. 1

    Add what you know

    Paste usernames, emails, names, domains, phones or IPs. Record who authorised the case and why, or start from a template.

  2. 2

    Scan and correlate

    Tools run in parallel. Account hits are checked against their profile pages, duplicates merged, and every lead followed up automatically.

  3. 3

    Review and report

    Confirm or rule out findings one key at a time, write your assessment, and export a report, CSV, JSON or STIX 2.1.

Trust and responsibility

Careful use is the default

Authorised first

No case without a written authorisation and a lawful basis. The database itself enforces it.

Encrypted at rest

Identifiers, findings, notes and API keys are encrypted, with keys kept away from the database.

Everything on the record

Every decision, export and share-link view is logged against a named person.

Nothing kept forever

Each case has a retention period and is deleted when it ends.
