unmask orchestrates open-source intelligence tools into repeatable investigations: automated pivot chains, case management with a full audit trail, and diffs between scans so you see what changed — not just what exists.

Sign in How it works

Built for analysts, not dashboards

Automated pivot chains

A username surfaces an email, the email triggers breach and domain lookups. Every automatic step is written to a pivot log you can read line by line.

Case management & audit trail

Each case requires a written authorization note and a lawful-basis confirmation before a single lookup runs. Every view, merge and export is logged.

Time-aware diffing

Scans are versioned. Compare any two runs to see new, changed and vanished findings — and whether a disappearance is real or a tool that failed.

Explainable correlation

Confidence is scored per field and every merge carries a plain-language explanation. Source reliability is rated separately from identity confidence.

Honest failures

Bot walls and soft-200 pages are detected by content signature, so a blocked tool never masquerades as "nothing found". Failing tools trip a circuit breaker.

Open adapter architecture

Every tool plugs in through one small interface and runs as an isolated subprocess or API call. Add a source without touching the core.

Principles

  • Encryption at rest. Emails, phone numbers and breach data are encrypted before they reach the database, with keys held outside it.
  • Humans conclude. Reports cannot be exported without a written analyst assessment — the platform presents evidence, a person states the finding.
  • Nothing invisible. Every scan failure, automatic pivot and manual override is recorded and shown.
