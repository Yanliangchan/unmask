{# --- 01 The problem: a ledger, before and after ----------------------------------------------- #}

§01 · The problem

Twenty tabs is not an investigation.

Ad-hoc OSINT

  • No record of who approved the work
  • A blocked tool read as "nothing found"
  • Two accounts judged the same, no one knows why
  • Screenshots that can't be checked later

A case file

  • Authorisation and lawful basis, written before the first lookup
  • Every failure shown as a failure, with the reason
  • Every match with its evidence for and against
  • Captured pages with their SHA-256 fingerprint
{# --- 02 Method --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- #}

§02 · Method

Five steps, all of them on the record.

  1. 1

    Authorise

    Name the identifiers, who approved the work and why. The case can't exist without it.

  2. 2

    Collect

    Tools run in parallel within their limits. Profile pages are checked; a bot wall is a failure, never "nothing found".

  3. 3

    Correlate

    Identical values merge. Anything that is a judgement is a suggestion with its reason, for a person to decide.

  4. 4

    Follow up, within limits

    Strong findings are followed up automatically, capped by depth and budget. Every skipped follow-up says why.

  5. 5

    Decide and report

    Review findings one at a time, write your assessment, and send the report.

{# --- 03 Evidence -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- #}

§03 · Evidence

Evidence, not verdicts.

Every score shows its working

What raised it (a checked profile page, independent sources, a link from another profile) and what lowered it, in plain words on every row. Source grade is kept apart from the match.

Every link says how it was made

Observed by a tool, matched by a rule, suggested by similarity, or decided by a person. Inferred links are labelled as inferred.

Paths with their weakest link

Ask how two findings connect and get the strongest evidence paths, each naming the link it rests on.

Decisions at the speed of reading

Review one finding at a time beside what you know, with the keyboard or on a phone.

{# --- 05 Measured -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- #}

§05 · Measured

Measured, not asserted.

The matching engine runs on every change against labelled cases built around how attribution goes wrong: namesakes, pages that echo any username, other people's accounts that link to each other, common handles, reordered names.

{# --- 06 Governance, set as the form a reviewer signs ----------------------------------------- #}

§06 · Governance

Built for the people who sign off.

Security, HR and counsel can see who approved each case, what was searched, what came back and who concluded what, long after the case itself is gone.

How data is protected · Acceptable use

Form A-1Authorisation record

Authorised first
A written authorisation and lawful basis, checked in the interface, the service and the database.
Bounded
At most 25 identifiers about one subject, and daily limits per account. Hitting one is logged.
On the record
Sign-ins, views, scans, decisions and exports, against a named person. The log outlives the case.
Gated output
No report or export without a written assessment. Share links are read-only and expire.
Not kept forever
