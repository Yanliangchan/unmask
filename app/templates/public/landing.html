00

Twenty tabs is not an investigation

Ad-hoc OSINT leaves nothing anyone can review: no record of who approved the work, which tools failed and were read as "nothing found", or why two accounts were judged to be the same person. {{ brand.NAME }} turns the same work into a case file: what was searched, what came back, how each conclusion was reached, and who reached it.

01

Method

  1. Authorise

    Name the subject's identifiers, who approved the work and why. The case cannot exist without it.

  2. Collect

    Tools run in parallel within their rate limits. Account hits are checked against the live profile page; a bot wall is reported as a failure, never as an empty result.

  3. Correlate

    Identical identifiers merge. Everything that is a judgement (similar names, matching handles) is a suggestion with its reason, for a person to accept or reject.

  4. Pivot, within limits

    Strong findings are followed up automatically, capped by depth and budget. Every skipped pivot is logged with the reason.

  5. Decide and report

    Confirm or rule out findings one key at a time, write the assessment, and export. Watch mode re-scans and shows what changed.

02

Evidence, not verdicts

Every score shows its working

A finding's score lists what raised it (a checked profile page, independent tools, a link from another profile, the case context) and what lowered it (a page that names someone else, a common handle). Source grade (Admiralty A–F) is kept apart from the match score.

Every link says how it was made

Observed by a tool, matched by a rule, suggested by similarity, or decided by an analyst, with when it was first and last seen. Inferred links are drawn and labelled as inferred.

Paths with their weakest link

Ask how two findings connect and get the strongest evidence paths, ranked by link kind and confidence, discounted through shared hubs, each naming the link it rests on.

Pages kept as they were

Capture a profile with its SHA-256 fingerprint so the evidence survives when the page changes. Every sighting records which tool reported it, from what, and when.

03

Measured, not asserted

The Identity Resolution Lab runs the real correlation engine on labelled cases built around how attribution goes wrong: namesakes, pages that echo any username, another person's accounts that link to each other, common handles, foreign phone numbers, reordered names. It runs on every change.

04

Built for the people who sign off

Authorised first
No case without a written authorisation and a lawful basis, checked in the interface, the service and a database constraint.
Bounded
A case holds at most 25 identifiers about one subject; each account has daily limits on targets and scans, and hitting one is logged.
On the record
Sign-ins, case views, scans, decisions, exports and share-link views are logged against a named person, and the log outlives the case.
Gated output
Reports and data exports need a written analyst assessment first. Share links are read-only and expire within 30 days.
Nothing kept forever
Each case has a retention period and is deleted when it lapses.
