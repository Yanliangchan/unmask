Limits

Scores combine the source's starting estimate, independent sources that agree, links between fields and matches to the case's context. Only identical identifiers are merged automatically; identity matches are decided by an analyst. A tool that could not complete is reported as failed, never as having found nothing. Open-source information can be wrong, out of date or planted: a finding is evidence about attribution, not proof of identity.
